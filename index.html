Emotion 1 — Happiness

Happiness is a positive emotion experienced when good things happen, leading to feelings of joy, contentment, and satisfaction.

Happiness is a positive energy that broadens your perspective!

Emotion 2 — Sadness

Sadness is a natural response to loss, disappointment, or feeling hurt, signaling a need for comfort and reflection.

"It's okay not to be okay."

Emotion 3 — Fear

Fear is an anxious emotional reaction triggered by perceived threats or danger, designed to protect us from harm.

Our brain's alarm system!

Emotion 4 — Anger

Anger is an intense emotional response to unfairness, frustration, or boundaries being crossed.

Use this energy for positive change.

Emotion 5 — Surprise

Surprise is a brief, spontaneous reaction to an unexpected event or sudden turn of circumstances.

Focus on processing visual info quickly!

Emotion 6 — Disgust

Disgust is a strong emotional rejection or aversion toward unpleasant, harmful, or offensive things.

Protecting us from what is "bad".
